# Shora App

This app is designed for people in a specific geographic area who select representatives to manage their homes, land, and public affairs. It aims to promote transparency and accountability, as well as establish a sub-department to assist with vulnerability issues.

The app is intended for Shora in Iran, where each village has its own Nemayande Shora and a defined population. It will function like a small government, prioritizing humanitarian values and fostering progress in all aspects of life through engagement and contribution.

## Key Features

*   Representative Selection: Allows users to select their Nemayande Shora.
*   Transparency and Accountability: Provides a platform for open communication and tracking of decisions.
*   Vulnerability Assistance: Includes a sub-department dedicated to addressing vulnerability issues within the community.
*   Community Engagement: Facilitates engagement and contributions from community members to foster progress.

## Target Audience

The primary users of this application are the residents of villages in Iran that utilize the Shora system.

## Goals

*   To empower local communities by providing a tool for better governance.
*   To enhance transparency in local decision-making processes.
*   To support vulnerable populations through dedicated assistance programs.
*   To encourage active participation and contribution from all community members towards collective progress.
